Completed: all 30-seed v1 diagnostics; two binary risk policies; continuous-ductility CBO with and without a risk threshold; unchanged four baselines; frozen exploratory protocol; 7,800 evaluation selections including initialization/threshold sensitivities and grouped stress tests; 900 common-history shadow forecasts; full 3,600-selection primary reproduction; 124 passing tests.

Ordinary CBO retains the highest mean discovery AUC. None of the four new branches meets both prespecified engineering goals. Every new branch receives **NO CONFIRMED IMPROVEMENT**. The binary risk policies reduce failures but lose too much discovery performance and nearly always require their minimum-risk fallback. Continuous modeling worsens calibration on identical CBO histories. All findings are conditional on the reused 80-case cohort; no independent-dataset improvement is claimed.
