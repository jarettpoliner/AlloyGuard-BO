# AlloyGuard-BO v2 — prespecified exploratory protocol

The entire published Citrine cohort has already been examined in v1. No pristine independent dataset is available. All v2 comparisons, including group stress tests, are exploratory. Replay-seed confidence intervals quantify initialization/selection variability conditional on these 80 cases, not population or dataset generalization. No risk parameter search, development optimization, or best-policy selection is performed. Each experimental branch receives its own verdict.

Primary outcome remains trapezoidal best-confirmed feasible yield strength over steps 0–15, including the matched 12-case initialization. Primary constraint is measured elongation >=10%. Thirty matched seeds 0–29 and all 80 qualified cases reproduce v1. All four baselines use unmodified v1 functions. Strength models and input features remain unchanged. Failures count only the 15 additional selections. Initial incumbent contributes to AUC, so 98% retention is potentially permissive.

Eight policies: random, strength BO, ordinary CBO, reliability-calibrated CBO, risk-threshold CBO, risk-budget BO, continuous-ductility CBO (CD-CBO), and CD-CBO with the same risk threshold. New policies use raw model feasibility probabilities. Risk threshold is fixed at .75, an engineering preference for predicted failure probability <=25%. Risk budget is 15*.25 =3.75 predicted failures, with remaining budget divided by remaining steps. These values are not selected from final results. Restrict CEI maximization to eligible candidates; if none qualify, minimize predicted risk, break ties by CEI then sorted case ID, continue all 15 experiments and explicitly log overruns. No guarantee on actual failure count. Past expenditure uses predictions stored before revelation, never realized failures or retrospectively recomputed predictions. Cold start with observed cases but no feasible incumbent uses v1 probability plus geometric exploration within the eligible pool. Empty initialization is unsupported, as in v1.

Continuous ductility uses a GP for log1p(measured percent elongation), same observed-only scaling and Matérn regression implementation as strength. Fixed log-response observation noise .15 and scale floor .5 are untuned assumptions. Kernel parameters optimize training marginal likelihood only, using the unchanged v1 kernel/bounds. P(E>=t)=Phi((mu-log1p(t))/observation_sd). Use observation uncertainty, not latent uncertainty. Negative inverse-transformed mass is censored to zero; probability at positive thresholds is unaffected. There is no physical 100% upper elongation bound. Audit predictive coverage, PIT, negative mass and skewness; retain failures of the distributional assumptions. Strength and ductility independence in CEI is an approximation.

Primary engineering goals for EACH new policy relative to ordinary CBO: mean failures <=.85 baseline and mean discovery AUC >=.98 baseline. Goals are not significance tests. IMPROVED TRADE-OFF means both point-estimate goals met (explicitly exploratory); NO CONFIRMED IMPROVEMENT means a paired 95% interval entirely violates at least one goal; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE. Compute paired contrasts failure_new-.85*failure_CBO and AUC_new-.98*AUC_CBO; report unadjusted t intervals across seeds and multiplicity limitations. Also report absolute paired differences and mean ratios; do not promote a policy after results.

Secondary comparisons: all policy means/95% seed intervals for AUC, confirmed strength, failures, Brier and log loss. Descriptive nondominated frontier across eight fixed policies (not a tuned continuous parameter sweep). Factorial ablation CBO vs CD-CBO vs threshold CBO vs CD-threshold isolates continuous modeling and threshold acquisition. Forecast scores on differing selected cases are selection-confounded: fit a continuous shadow model on ordinary CBO's exact observed histories, predict its selected case before revelation, compare against its logged binary forecast. Report Brier, log loss, ROC ranking and reliability overall and near measured elongation 5–15%; this band is diagnostic only, never an acquisition input.

Sensitivity: initial sizes 8 and16, thresholds8% and12%, each five seeds100–104 and all eight policies. Fixed models/risk settings, no tuning. Outcome-blind study-or-composition component holdouts use existing three folds, permutation seed0, seeds200–204. Initialize with12 randomly sampled outside-target cases, query15 inside-target cases. Require >=12 training cases and >=15 target cases; report support without outcome-driven repartition. At fold with exactly15 targets, final strength is exhausted-pool order-independent. Incumbent includes initial training cases. These stress tests are sequential adaptation to heldout groups, not fully untouched supervised test evaluations. Within a target group repeated observations can inform later choices.

v1 diagnostics use all30 seeds: compare failure/AUC paired intervals, failed probabilities in [.4,.6] and >=.75, expected vs actual failure totals, strength/EI/probability distributions and realized strength–elongation association. High probabilities among failed cases alone do not establish overconfidence; use outcome-conditioned reliability and forecast risk residuals. No causal claim from retrospective association.

Freeze config, implementation, tests, lock and cohort hashes before final comparisons. Audit separately from selection: recompute measurements, pre-reveal IDs, risk arithmetic, curves/metrics, baseline equality, preservation, and reproduction. Synthetic tests are correctness evidence only. Preserve all negative findings and v1 files.
