# Scientific limitations and closure

The completed Citrine v1/v2 study is exploratory and retrospective. It is not a prospective laboratory validation, a synthesis-success predictor, or evidence of state-of-the-art improvement. The earlier GE ordinal-screening experiment and provisional Mendeley work answer different or incomplete qualification questions. Historical folders named `strength_v3` do not mean a new v3 campaign has started.

- **Independence:** 80 reported cases span 36 studies, 68 chemistries and 31 connected study-or-chemistry groups. These are not 80 proven independent measurements. Thirty replay seeds quantify initialization variability conditional on one pool, not 30 independent datasets. Calibration and shift splits respect groups, but primary sequential replay can observe multiple cases from the same study. Possible development-dataset overlap exists for 63 cases.
- **Qualification and representativeness:** master/source fields explicitly identify tensile tests at 25°C. Ambiguous repeated composition/recipe keys are excluded wholesale. Recipe names do not fully specify annealing temperatures, strain rates or material states. Selection of fully documented paired records may bias the cohort.
- **Targets and features:** measured yield strength and elongation are paired outcomes. Composition-only predictors omit important processing information. Feasibility is a measured >=10% elongation requirement, not a synthesis or safety outcome. No measurement censoring is presented as exact data; no missing temperatures are inferred.
- **Surrogates:** strength observation noise is assumed 25 MPa, not a per-experiment uncertainty estimate. GP kernel boundaries and interval undercoverage persist. Binary classifier probabilities often cluster around the boundary; continuous log1p-GP predictions exhibit undercoverage and confidently wrong constraint estimates. Product EI × probability does not model a validated joint strength/ductility distribution.
- **Risk rules:** .75 minimum predicted feasibility is rarely attainable; both binary policies use fallback in 98% or more of queries. All risk-budget runs exceed the predicted budget; it is a soft allocation rule with an explicit fallback, not a hard safety guarantee. Observed failure savings do not repair forecast-budget infeasibility.
- **Metrics:** AUC includes the 12-case initialization; the unchanged initial incumbent would supply 85% of CBO AUC. Failures exclude initialization. At 72.5% baseline feasibility, a feasible incumbent is almost certain. AUC rewards early incumbent strength, not terminal utility, money or material deployment value. Utility penalties are externally defined preference conversions.
- **Statistics:** paired seed t intervals are conditional, unadjusted and non-simultaneous. Mean Pareto dominance is not statistical dominance. CBO's mean advantage over random is uncertain; calibration and the two binary risk methods are not meaningfully distinguishable. Break-even ratio estimates have substantial uncertainty. The 5/10-query utility analyses are prefixes of 15-query plans, not fresh horizon-aware evaluations.
- **Generalization:** grouped target pools contain only 15–34 cases, one is exhausted, and initialization can dominate discovery. More seeds do not add independent observations. Tuning additional algorithms on these already exposed results risks researcher selection bias.

All v2 policies fail the frozen joint engineering goal (>=15% fewer failures and >=98% CBO AUC). These thresholds were not significance tests. The decision is to close and publish the benchmark with negative/trade-off findings. A future independent study would require a separate hypothesis, development-only parameter selection and new evaluation evidence; no such study is implemented here.

See `results/decision_review/DECISION_REPORT.md` and its technical appendix for quantified evidence, and `DATA_LICENSES.md` for the distinct historical-data rights limitations. The audits are separate computational verification paths, not external human peer review.
